Overview

An online booking platform for our rental fleet

booKING lets customers find, price and book a car online in minutes, and gives staff one place to manage the fleet, locations and prices.

The 30-second pitch
  • Customers book and pay online, any time, with no phone calls or emails.
  • Prices are calculated automatically from our own rules: seasons, rental length, location fees and tax.
  • Staff manage cars, plates, locations and prices from a simple admin panel.
  • A working prototype already exists. The next step is making it production-ready.

Features

What the product does today, and what it will do at launch.

Built Already working in the prototype

  • Fleet browsing: customers search and browse available models with specs and a live price quote.
  • Online booking: choose pick-up and drop-off location and dates, enter name and email, and book.
  • Payment step: checkout flow is complete, currently with simulated payment.
  • No double bookings: two customers can never get the same car for the same dates.
  • 15-minute hold: a car is reserved while the customer pays, then released if they don't.
  • Secure booking links: each customer gets a private link to view their booking.
  • Tamper-proof prices: prices are always calculated on the server, so customers can't edit them.
  • Smart pricing engine:
    • Cheaper daily rate for longer rentals (e.g. from day 4, from day 8).
    • Seasonal surcharges or discounts (summer, holidays, off-season).
    • Location surcharges and pick-up / drop-off fees (e.g. airport).
    • Tax added on top or included in prices.
    • Every booking keeps its price, even if rules change later.
  • Admin panel: manage car models, individual cars (plates, features, home location) and locations.
  • Availability overview: 14-day calendar per model plus a list of its bookings.

Planned Included before launch

  • Real card payments through a payment provider.
  • Confirmation emails with booking summary and link.
  • Cancellations: by staff, and optionally by customers under set rules.
  • Abuse protection: limits so nobody can block cars with fake reservations.
  • Safe data updates: data is kept intact when the system is upgraded.
  • Automated testing: every change is checked before it goes live.
  • Deactivate instead of delete for cars and locations with booking history.
  • Proper staff login with individual accounts.
